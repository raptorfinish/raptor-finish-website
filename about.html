Who We Are

A Premium Mobile Detailing Brand

Raptor Finish is a premium mobile auto detailing business based in Metro Vancouver — serving Surrey, White Rock, the Lower Mainland, and the Fraser Valley. We don't operate out of a fixed location. We come to you, by appointment.

We created Raptor Finish because we saw a gap between basic car washes and the level of care that serious vehicle owners actually want. Our work is detailed, precise, and built around delivering a result that speaks for itself.

Our long-term vision is to grow into luxury, performance, and exotic vehicle detailing — and eventually, private aviation. Right now, our focus is simple: earn your trust through quality work. View our full list of services or book your detail today.
