Who We Are

Precision. Protection.
Transformation.

Raptor Finish is a premium mobile auto detailing and commercial fleet washing business serving Metro Vancouver and the Lower Mainland. We built this business around one belief: your vehicle deserves more than a basic wash.

We don't operate out of a shop. Every service is delivered at your location — your house or your yard. We bring the water, the power, the products, and the expertise.

From daily drivers and luxury cars to semi trucks, trailers, and heavy equipment, every vehicle gets the same relentless attention to detail. That's not a slogan — it's the standard.
