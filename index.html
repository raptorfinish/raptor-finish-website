What We Do

Premium Detailing Services

Every service is performed with precision, the right tools, and a relentless focus on quality. We come to you.

01

Premium Interior Detail

A deep, thorough interior clean that eliminates dirt, odours, and grime — leaving every surface, fibre, and trim looking and feeling showroom-fresh.

  • Deep vacuum — seats, carpet, mats, trunk
  • Full interior wipe-down and dressing
  • Window cleaning (inside)
  • Door panels, dash, and console detail
  • Odour treatment

02

Exterior Detail

A precision exterior clean that restores gloss, removes contamination, and protects your paint surface against the elements.

  • Hand wash and rinse
  • Clay bar decontamination
  • Wheel and tire cleaning
  • Window cleaning (outside)
  • Tyre dressing
Most Popular

03

Full Detail Package

The complete Raptor Finish experience. Interior and exterior combined — your vehicle fully transformed inside and out.

  • Full interior detail
  • Full exterior detail
  • Engine bay wipe-down
  • Trim and rubber restoration
  • Final inspection and walkthrough

04

Paint Enhancement

A single-stage machine polish that removes light swirls, water spots, and minor surface defects — restoring clarity and gloss to the paint.

  • Wash and decontamination
  • Single-stage machine polish
  • Panel wipe and prep
  • Finishing wax or sealant application
Advanced

05

Paint Correction

Multi-stage machine polishing that removes deep swirl marks, scratches, and oxidation — restoring the paint to a flawless, mirror-like finish.

  • Full wash and clay decontamination
  • Multi-stage machine correction
  • Panel inspection under lighting
  • Final polish and protection
Premium Protection

06

Ceramic Coating

The ultimate long-term paint protection. A professional-grade ceramic coating that bonds to your paint, delivering years of gloss and hydrophobic protection.

  • Full paint decontamination and correction
  • Surface prep and panel wipe
  • Professional ceramic coating application
  • Curing and final inspection
  • Maintenance guide provided

07

Engine Bay Detail

A careful and thorough engine bay clean that removes grease, grime, and buildup — making your engine bay look as sharp as the exterior.

  • Degreaser application and rinse
  • Detail brush cleaning
  • Plastic and rubber dressing
  • Final wipe-down and inspection

Raptor Finish was built on one belief — your vehicle deserves more than a basic clean. It deserves precision, care, and a result you can be proud of.

Who We Are

A Premium Mobile Detailing Brand

Raptor Finish is a premium mobile auto detailing business based in the Metro Vancouver area — serving Surrey, White Rock, the Lower Mainland, and the Fraser Valley. We don't operate out of a fixed location. We come to you.

We created Raptor Finish because we saw a gap between basic car washes and the level of care that serious vehicle owners actually want. Our work is detailed, precise, and built around delivering a result that speaks for itself.

Our Mission

Precision in Every Pass

Every vehicle we work on is treated with the same standard — whether it's a daily driver or a performance car. We take the time to do it right. No shortcuts. No rushed jobs. Just clean, professional detailing work delivered to your door.

Our long-term vision is to grow into luxury, performance, and exotic vehicle detailing — and eventually, private aviation. But right now, our focus is simple: earn your trust through quality work.
