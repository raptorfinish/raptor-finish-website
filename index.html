Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas does Raptor Finish serve?

We serve Surrey, White Rock, Langley, Delta, Burnaby, Richmond, and the greater Metro Vancouver and Fraser Valley area in BC.

How long does a full detail take?

A full interior and exterior detail typically takes 3 to 5 hours depending on vehicle size and condition.

Do I need to be home during the detail?

No. As long as we have access to your vehicle and a safe place to work, you do not need to be present.

What is ceramic coating and how long does it last?

Ceramic coating is a professional-grade liquid polymer that bonds to your paint, providing 2 to 5 years of protection against UV, water, and contaminants.

How do I book a mobile detail with Raptor Finish?

Fill out our online booking form, call (604) 477-7700, or message us on WhatsApp.

How much does mobile auto detailing cost?

Prices start from $80 for an engine bay detail and $200+ for a full detail package. Final price depends on vehicle size and condition. Contact us for a free quote.
