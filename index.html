Common Questions

FAQ

What areas does Raptor Finish serve?

We serve all of Metro Vancouver and the Lower Mainland, BC. We are fully mobile — we come to your house or yard.

Do you wash trucks and trailers?

Yes. We wash and detail semi trucks, transport trailers, dump trucks, box trucks, flat decks, and work vans. Truck washing is done at your yard, depot, or job site anywhere in Metro Vancouver and the Lower Mainland. Contact us for a quote.

Do you offer mobile fleet washing?

Yes. Mobile fleet washing is one of our core commercial services. We come to your yard on a schedule that suits your operation — weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly — so your entire fleet stays clean and road-ready without any vehicle leaving the lot.

How much does truck washing cost?

Commercial truck and fleet washing is quoted individually. Pricing depends on vehicle size, condition, number of units, and service frequency. Fleet accounts typically receive better per-unit rates. Contact us for a free quote.

Can you wash heavy equipment and machinery?

Yes. We degrease and pressure wash excavators, skid steers, forklifts, and other heavy equipment — including cab clean-outs and undercarriage rinses. This is popular for pre-sale prep, auction listings, and end-of-project cleanups.

Do you come to our yard or job site?

Yes. All commercial work is performed on site at your yard, depot, or active job site. There is no need to send drivers or equipment anywhere — we bring everything required to complete the job.

Do I need to provide water or power?

No. We arrive fully equipped with our own water, power, products, and tools for both auto detailing and commercial fleet washing.

How long does a full detail take?

A full interior and exterior detail typically takes 3 to 5 hours depending on vehicle size and condition. Commercial washes vary by unit count and are confirmed at quoting.

Do I need to be present during the service?

No. As long as we have access to the vehicle or yard and a safe place to work, you don't need to be on site.

How do I book?

Call (604) 477-7700, message us on WhatsApp, or email hello@raptorfinish.com. Commercial and fleet enquiries are welcome.
