Raptor Finish — Premium Mobile Auto Detailing in Metro Vancouver

Raptor Finish delivers precision auto detailing that restores, protects, and elevates vehicles to a showroom-quality finish. We are a mobile detailing service based in Surrey and White Rock, BC — serving the entire Metro Vancouver and Lower Mainland area.

We come to you. Whether you're at home, at work, or anywhere in between, our detailing team arrives fully equipped to transform your vehicle. No drop-off required. No waiting at a shop. Just precision results, brought to your driveway. You can also reach us instantly via WhatsApp. All bookings are by appointment — book your detail today and we'll take care of the rest.

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas does Raptor Finish serve?

We serve Surrey, White Rock, Langley, Delta, Burnaby, Richmond, and the greater Metro Vancouver and Fraser Valley area in British Columbia. We are a fully mobile service and come to your location.

How long does a full detail take?

A full interior and exterior detail typically takes 3 to 5 hours depending on the size and condition of your vehicle. We'll give you a more accurate estimate when you book.

Do I need to be home during the detail?

No. As long as we have access to your vehicle and a safe place to work, you do not need to be present during the detail. Many of our clients book and go about their day.

What is ceramic coating and how long does it last?

Ceramic coating is a professional-grade liquid polymer applied to the exterior of your vehicle that chemically bonds to the paint. It provides long-term protection against UV damage, water, and contaminants. A professional ceramic coating typically lasts 2 to 5 years depending on how well it is maintained.

How do I book a mobile detail with Raptor Finish?

Simply fill out our online booking form, call us at (604) 477-7700, or message us on WhatsApp. All bookings are by appointment and we come directly to your location.

How much does mobile auto detailing cost?

Pricing varies depending on the service, vehicle size, and condition. Contact us for a personalized quote — we'll assess your vehicle's needs and provide a clear price before any work begins.
